# BitByte Innovations

**Technology, design, and digital growth, one connected team.**

Based in Chittagong, Bangladesh, we help businesses build useful software and reach the people who need it. Our engineers, designers, and marketing specialists work together from the first conversation through launch and ongoing improvement.

## What we do

- **Technology & development:** Websites, mobile apps, custom business software, UI/UX, cloud, and AI solutions.
- **Organic search:** Technical, local, and e-commerce SEO, plus AI search optimization.
- **Content & video:** Video production, written content, branding, and graphic design.
- **Digital marketing:** Social media strategy, content planning, and customer engagement.
- **Paid media:** Search and social advertising, creative testing, and campaign reporting.
